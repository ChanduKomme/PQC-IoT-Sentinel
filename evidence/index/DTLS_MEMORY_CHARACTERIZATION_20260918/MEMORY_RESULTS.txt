minimum-ever free heap changed from 49776 to 49744 bytes (32 bytes).

recorded repeated-session samples.

Important limitation
--------------------
The PQC runtime-memory measurements cover the completed 24-hour endurance
experiment. The DTLS runtime-memory measurements come from a short
separate repeated-session characterization run. They therefore provide
observed implementation-level resource evidence, but are not equal-duration
peak-memory benchmarks.

Minimum-ever-free heap values indicate allocator low-water marks. The
difference from post-session free heap should not be interpreted as
memory attributable to one specific cryptographic operation.
